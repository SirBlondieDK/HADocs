## R004-F-001 — collision-detection scope

**PASS — RESOLVED**

The corrected normative specification now requires collision detection:

- before publication of every derived reference;
- installation-wide within the exact identity context comprising format
  version, installation scope and secret generation;
- against a durable private registry covering every canonical tuple previously
  accepted or attempted in that context;
- across current snapshots and retained or deleted historical snapshots;
- through an atomic compare-and-register operation;
- for the lifetime of the usable identity context, independent of snapshot
  deletion.

The registry binds the complete public reference to the exact canonical tuple.
Same-reference/same-tuple repetition is valid; same-reference/different-tuple
is a collision. Registry contents remain internal sensitive state and may not
be exported or logged.

Collision, unavailable registry, corrupt registry or inability to perform the
atomic comparison all fail closed: the affected new capability snapshot and
dependent observations/relationships are rejected, no new protected reference
is emitted, no suffix/order repair is allowed, and only a last valid immutable
snapshot may remain explicitly stale.

The scope and response leave no implementation-selected collision-detection
boundary. Storage technology remains an implementation choice, but required
behavior and lifetime are normative.

## R004-F-002 — secret-generation wording

**PASS — RESOLVED**

The HMAC primitive and exact 32-octet key length are unchanged.
