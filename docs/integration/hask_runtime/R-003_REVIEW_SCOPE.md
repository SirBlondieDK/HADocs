# R-003 Review Scope

R-003 independently reviews AI-001’s proposed definitions of `canonical_key`, `installation_scope`, `source_capability`, `observation_id`, and only their necessary relationship, stability, privacy, collision and versioning interactions.

- Initiating evidence: I-001B_RESUME identity-contract blocker.
- Governance: G-001.
- Active baseline: DF-002 and its frozen contract documents.
- Proposal: AI-001; not authoritative unless later frozen.
- Approval standard: complete, unambiguous, internally consistent, DF-002-compatible, privacy-preserving and independently reproducible.
- Rejection/change standard: any unresolved normative or compatibility defect.
- Blocker standard: missing authority or evidence prevents a valid review decision.

R-003 may identify defects but may not repair, redesign, freeze or implement them. It may not change scope counts, predicates, contract version, production code, tests, fixtures, HASK, Consumer Contract, PI2 or historical governance.

An approval would permit only DF-003. It would not authorize implementation. I-001B_RESUME_2 may begin only after DF-003; V-001 remains required after implementation.
