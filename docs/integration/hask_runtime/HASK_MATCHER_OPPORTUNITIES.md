# HASK Matcher Opportunities

No matchers are designed here. High-value future evidence domains are:

- configuration and trigger/condition/action validation;
- target resolution and missing-reference integrity;
- entity/device/area registry consistency;
- loaded-component and registered-service presence;
- explicit assistant-exposure configuration;
- event registration and listener-count facts;
- calendar entity inventory where privacy policy permits.

These opportunities require a separate knowledge increment with claim-level provenance and exact native-field contracts. State values, histories and event payloads must not be converted directly into causes. Connectivity has no opportunity under the current documented surface.
